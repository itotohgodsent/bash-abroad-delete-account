Bash Abroad - Account Deletion

This page explains how users of the Bash Abroad app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Bash Abroad app and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile and check Settings for the Delete Account option, if available.
  3. If you cannot delete your account in the app, contact Bash Abroad support through the app's support or reporting feature and request permanent account deletion.
  4. Include your account username so support can identify your account. Do not send your password.

Data deletion

Once your identity and request are verified, Bash Abroad will process deletion of your account and associated personal information, including profile information and user-generated content, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements.

Data retention

Some transaction, security, or compliance records may need to be retained where legally required. Applicable retention periods will depend on the type of record and legal requirements.

For questions, use the support or reporting feature in the Bash Abroad app.
